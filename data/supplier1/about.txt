Our organization established in the year 1976 the name called Bharat Industrial Supplier are accredited manufacturer, supplier, distributor, exporter, trader, wholesaler and retailer of Fasteners. Being an ISO 9001:2015 firm, we are offering best quality Steel Fasteners, Metal Fasteners and Industrial Fasteners. We have attained remarkable success in this domain with our broad range of products which are procured from authentic vendors. These vendors manufacture them by using finest grade raw material and modernistic technology. In addition to this we are providing world class Industrial Fabrication services as per the exact requirement of the clients.


With the support of world-class infrastructure and extensive vendor base network, we are able to fulfill diversified requirements of customers with uttermost perfection. We have agents for selling our projects all over the world and we are specialized in manufacturer and exporter of Industrial Equipment's. We supply our qualitative assortment of products to customers which are procured from reputed vendors such as HILTI, FISCHER, PATTA, HP. Moreover, we are having branches in Korea, China, Hongkong and Taiwan.


Under the adroit guidance of Mr. Kutbudeen M, we have achieved remarkable success in this domain through his competent managing and marketing skills. His primal goal of attaining maximum quality at all clients' dealing have awarded us vast clientele base to serve.
